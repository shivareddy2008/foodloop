How FoodLoop Works in 4 Steps

A frictionless closed loop designed for university campuses, catering hubs, and municipal rescue operations.

🍳
Step 1

Donor Lists Surplus

Mess warden, hotel chef, or event manager posts surplus food details with servings, packaging type, and safe pickup deadline in under 60 seconds.

📡
Step 2

Live Radar Alert

Surplus batch instantly appears on the real-time Leaflet map. Nearby NGOs, food banks, and student volunteer brigades receive alert radar beacons.

🔐
Step 3

Claim & Secure OTP

Volunteer claims the pickup, inputs vehicle and ETA, and receives a digital 4-digit verification code. The donor verifies this code upon handover.

✨
Step 4

Safe Redistribution

Meals reach shelter homes, street families, or campus night staff while piping hot and within the FSSAI safety window. Zero edible waste!

Food Safety & Redistribution Protocol

FoodLoop strictly adheres to the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) Surplus Food Regulations.

🌡️

Temperature Control

Cooked hot meals must be kept above 60°C in insulated thermal vessels. Perishable cold food or dairy items must be stored below 5°C at all times during storage and transport.

⏱️

The 4-Hour Rule

Cooked surplus food must be collected and consumed within 4 hours of preparation unless professionally blast chilled. The platform countdown automatically locks unpicked food at the deadline.

📦

Hygienic Packaging

All donations must be sealed in clean, food-grade containers or biodegradable bagasse meal boxes. Rescuers carrying their own containers must ensure food-grade sanitization.

👃

Sensory Verification

Before entering the 4-digit handover OTP, volunteers conduct a visual, aroma, and texture inspection. Any food exhibiting sourness or surface moisture must be responsibly composted.

🎓 IP Project Evaluation & Viva Briefing

Project: FoodLoop — Smart Food Rescue Platform

Course: B.Tech Internet Programming (IP) Laboratory Project
Domain: Web Applications, Geolocation Routing, Hyper-Local Logistics & SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption)

1. Full Stack Architecture

Frontend Architecture:
  • Responsive Single Page Application (SPA)
  • Real Interactive Leaflet.js + OpenStreetMap
  • Zero Google Maps Paid Key Dependency
  • Glassmorphism, CSS Custom Properties & Micro-interactions
Backend & Persistence:
  • Node.js Native REST API Server (Zero NPM fragility)
  • Embedded SQLite (`foodloop.db`) with WAL mode
  • ACID Compliant Transactions & Foreign Keys
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Audit Logs

2. Key Technical Innovations for Viva Demonstration

  1. Interactive Map Dual Synchronization: Clicking map markers auto-scrolls and highlights listing cards, and vice versa.
  2. Digital Handover Protocol: Generates a cryptographically random 4-digit OTP upon claiming. Handover cannot complete until donor verifies the volunteer's code.
  3. Real-time Expiry Clocks: Dynamic recalculation of safe consumption windows with automated urgent alerts when < 2.5 hours remain.
  4. FSSAI Compliance Engine: Built-in temperature and packaging verification checklist.
